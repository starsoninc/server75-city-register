A register that can be changed quietly is not a register. Publishing through the repo gives every change an author, a timestamp and a diff, and the fingerprint at the top changes when the contents do. Any alliance can check the fingerprint they were shown against the one the page reports.

City buffs and coordinates

What a city gives whoever holds it, and where it sits. Any signed-in editor can fill these in — they are facts about the map, not decisions about the rotation. Until the Level 6 buffs are recorded, pick order decides nothing, because nobody can tell which fortress is worth choosing first.

Record a change of holder

For cities outside the rotation — a standing allocation, a released city being picked up, or a correction. The three rotating Level 6 cities are not listed here: they are assigned by the schedule, and the way to change who holds one is to change the pool, not to reassign the city.

Rules text

Markdown. Headings, lists, bold, italics and links render on the Rules tab. Changing the rules is published, dated and attributed like any other change — which is the point of keeping them here rather than in a pinned message that nobody can date.

Square-bracket placeholders like [DATE] are highlighted on the Rules tab so an unfinished rule looks unfinished.
