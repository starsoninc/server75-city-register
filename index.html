Two records, kept apart because they measure different things. Server health

How every alliance on #75 is doing, from one reading a night in the last hour before the reset (api.lastasylumtracker.com, via the WPP alliance dashboard). Active = a sign of play that server day: tech or honor donated, weekly activity points up, power up, or the base moved. Power change counts the same players only, so joiners and leavers never look like growth. KvK attendance = members whose kills went up in the KvK window, Saturday 15:00–17:00 Berlin (from 28 Nov; before: Friday night to Saturday night), of those read at both times. It is shown for information; it is not part of the KvK contribution standing.

AvA

Who each #75 alliance faces in AvA this week, and who to watch out for. Each alliance picks its opponent here once a week (server, then alliance; anyone can set or fix it, every change is listed below). From then on the opponent's roster is read by itself about every 4 hours from api.lastasylumtracker.com, so growth shows day over day. In AvA only the opponent alliance can come to us, so only those players are counted as threats. Everything is compared by hero might (total hero might): each server's top 100 come from lastasylum.gg's Hero Might Rank, the opponents' other members from the tracker. Below #75's top 100, your own figure is looked up when you pick yourself, or you type it in.

Edit and publish

Sign in below, make your changes, then publish. Edits live in this browser tab until you publish them — nothing saves by itself.

A register that can be changed quietly is not a register. Publishing through the repo gives every change an author, a timestamp and a diff, and the fingerprint at the top changes when the contents do. Any alliance can check the fingerprint they were shown against the one the page reports.

City buffs and coordinates

What a city gives whoever holds it, and where it sits. Any signed-in editor can fill these in — they are facts about the map, not decisions about the rotation. Until the Level 6 buffs are recorded, pick order decides nothing, because nobody can tell which fortress is worth choosing first.

Record a change of holder

For cities outside the rotation — a standing allocation, a released city being picked up, or a correction. The three rotating Level 6 cities are not listed here: they are assigned by the schedule, and the way to change who holds one is to change the pool, not to reassign the city.
