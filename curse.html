1. How the event scores (60-second version)

  • Points = Cursed Potions. Your Sanctuary standing in the zone earns 40/s = 72,000 per round with zero effort.
  • Gathering points (≈1 min each): Healing Kit 2,700 total (allowed under shield) · Beast Bone ~6,300 + 30/s while you hold it · Runestone ~15,600 + 120/s while you hold it. Bones/Runestones remove your shield.
  • An early Runestone is everything: one gathered at minute 2–4 is worth ~200,000 by the end of the round; each minute of delay costs ~7,200.
  • Red circle (0–100 tiles): no shields; only red-circle Sanctuaries can gather/scout red-circle points. Yellow circle (101–150): shields allowed.
  • Artifacts are lost if you shield, if you leave the zone, or if someone beats your Sanctuary. Earned potions are never lost. Enemy players can also hit your march on a point and take it.
  • Chests use the total of both rounds (15k / 30k / 60k / 100k … up to 600k). Rankings use your best single round.
Floor for everyone: just sitting in the zone for both full rounds = 144,000 potions = the first four chests, with no fighting at all.

Read: beatable outside HERA's top 5. Their second line is a target, not a threat.

#76 The softest target — and we know them

We beat #76 in KvK on 26 Sep and 31 Oct. Weakest heroes in the group: nobody at 90M+, 45 of their top 100 under 55M. Their whale WhereAreMyPills (174M power) has only 81.7M hero might — softer than he looks. Their real fighters are JustAri (87.5M) and PH4NTOM (86.3M).

Read: their Sanctuaries holding artifacts are our primary prey. #85/#91 will hunt them too — get there first.

4. Focus targets — who to hit

Rule: we fight for artifacts, not kills. Every attack should either take a Runestone/Bone out of an enemy Sanctuary or steal a point from an enemy march. Scout first — scouting shows how many artifacts a Sanctuary holds. Compare hero might, not power, before you hit.
  1. Enemy marches sitting on Runestones. Highest value, lowest risk: you take the point and its potions, and a gathering march is often lighter than the owner's home defence. Applies to all three servers.
  2. #76 Sanctuaries holding artifacts — NTX, EoC, STLY, WSH players with 50–70M hero might. Most of our core outsizes them. Primary target in both rounds.
  3. #91 second line holding artifacts — RAV, ASVA, CGR1, SK48, PPL players with 55–70M hero might. Avoid HERA's top 5.
  4. #85 only opportunistically — single marches on points where you clearly outsize them. Never their Sanctuaries unless several of us hit a confirmed artifact-holder together.

6. Strategy by strength

Use whichever number fits you — power and hero might (Profile → Heroes) line up roughly like this on #75. If your hero might puts you a bracket higher than your power, play the higher bracket.

Whales — 140M+ power · 85M+ hero might

  • Red circle near the throne, next to another #75 whale. Never alone.
  • Free every march; send them at Runestones from second 10. Goal: 2–3 Runestones by minute 10.
  • No shield, never leave the zone. Strong troops stay home; heal between hits.
  • Don't chase kills. Your job is to hold Runestones until the bell.
  • If one of #85's top 5 (hero might 97M+) parks next to you: relocate early (1 free move per 5 min inside the zone) — don't wait to be hit.

Core — 115–140M · 65–85M hero might

  • Red circle, beside a whale — you are their bodyguard and hunter.
  • Beast Bones early; Runestones when one drops close.
  • Keep one march free to snipe enemy marches on Runestones and to hit scouted #76 / #91-second-line Sanctuaries with artifacts.
  • Reinforce allied whales when they're attacked.

Field — 100–115M · 55–65M hero might

  • Inner edge of the yellow circle (around 101–120 tiles), in a group of #75 players.
  • Bones and Healing Kits nearby; points spawn in both circles every 30 s and a big drop comes every 3 min (10-second warning) — keep a march ready for it.
  • Hit #76 marches on points; leave #85 alone.
  • Threatened while holding bones? Calculate: if the attacker clearly outsizes you, shielding (losing the bones) beats losing troops and bones.

Developing — under 100M · under 55M hero

  • Yellow circle, shield up, stay all 30 minutes. That alone is 72,000 per round.
  • Gather Healing Kits only — allowed under shield. Don't touch Bones/Runestones (they drop your shield).
  • Two full rounds = 144,000+ → first four chests, zero risk.
  • Every #75 player in the zone also helps our alliance ranking.

8. Checklist before each round

  • All troops healed · all marches free (no gathering/rallies elsewhere) · a cheap single-hero march ready for Healing Kits.
  • Direct Relocate items on hand (they work during the 5-minute free-move cooldown).
  • Know your bracket and your buddy (whale ⇄ core). Agree in alliance chat who goes red, who stays yellow.
  • Remember: shield = artifacts gone; leaving the zone = artifacts gone; potions you've earned are always safe.
  • Last 3 minutes: only start gathers that will finish before the bell.
